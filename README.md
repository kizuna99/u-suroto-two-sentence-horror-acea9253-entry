A two-sentence horror story for the make.fun "Two-Sentence Horror" bounty. `main.py` (Python 3, no dependencies) writes the story — exactly two sentences, 214 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`. A film captioner finds a subtitle she never typed, timed to a silence in the film, and the thing in the empty doorway turns out to be listening to whoever reads it.
